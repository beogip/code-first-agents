Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 June 2026

code-first-agents.com is a documentation website. It has no user accounts, no contact forms, no newsletter, no payments, and it sets no cookies and uses no localStorage, no fingerprinting, and no advertising or cross-site tracking. The data described below is the limited, technical data inherent to serving any website.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Juan Gipponi, operator of this site. For any privacy request or question, open an issue at github.com/beogip/code-first-agents/issues.

What data is processed, by whom, and why

When you load a page, your browser necessarily sends your IP address and request headers (such as User-Agent and Referer) to the providers that host and deliver this site. We do not combine this data with anything else and we do not use it to identify you.

Recipient Role Data & purpose
GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., served via the Fastly CDN) Hosting provider / processor Origin hosting of the site files. Processes your IP and request headers to deliver the pages.
Cloudflare, Inc. CDN & reverse proxy / processor Delivers the site over its network and routes requests. Processes your IP and request headers to serve and protect the site.
Cloudflare Web Analytics Privacy-first analytics Aggregate, cookieless traffic measurement (page views, referrers, country, device type). It does not set cookies, does not store data on your device, and does not track you across sites.

Fonts

Fonts are self-hosted on this site. No font files are requested from Google Fonts or any other third party, so your IP address is not shared with a font provider.

Legal basis

The processing above relies on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in operating, securing, and understanding the basic usage of a public documentation site. The processing is minimal, expected by visitors, and involves no profiling.

International transfers

GitHub and Cloudflare are US-based providers, so serving the site may involve a transfer of your IP address to the United States. These transfers rely on the providers' certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 45–46 GDPR).

Retention

We do not maintain our own server logs or analytics database. Analytics are aggregate and not tied to you. Any technical logs held by GitHub and Cloudflare are retained and deleted according to their own policies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, and objection regarding your personal data, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because this site stores no identifying data and no cookies, there is normally nothing to access or delete on our side; for any request, contact us via the GitHub issues link above.
